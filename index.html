🍀Bundee Aki🍀

Why is he famous

Bundee Aki is famous for his exceptional power, ball-carrying ability,
and relentless defense as a world-class rugby center for both the Irish national team and his club, Connacht.

A little bit about him

He is born on April 7, 1990. Which makes him 35 years old.
He is married to Kayla Aki with his five children called Armani-Jade, Adrianna, Andronicus, Ailbhe and Áine. Bundee Aki and his wife, Kayla, were together for around a decade before marrying in November 2018 in their native New Zealand,
meaning they likely met in late 2008 or 2009, after Bundee had moved to Ireland to play rugby and had been together for about ten years when they wed.

When did he start his carer

Bundee Aki started playing rugby as a child, around five or six years old, but his entry into professional rugby was not considered early due to family commitments.
His professional career was a "longer journey than normal," as he had to take a break from the sport to work as a bank teller and support his young family.
